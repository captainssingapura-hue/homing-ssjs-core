# Broken declarations, one per rule

## Orphan

Words with no branch to belong to.

## Stray

A role filed under nothing.

## Strays

Holds a part whose role is filed under nothing.

## Stateful

Holds a label of its own, which no node may.

## Helped

A role that keeps a helper beside it.

## Vacant

Holds a title nothing plays.

## Nameless

Holds a caption in no role at all.

## Stutter

Names one role twice.

## Mirror

Lists itself among what it holds.

## Ping

Lists Pong among what it holds.

## Pong

Lists Ping among what it holds.

## Here.Badge

A badge declared here.

## There.Badge

A badge declared there.

## Elsewhere.Title

A second role that answers to title.

## Doubled

Names the second title.

## Another.Naming

A second branch that answers to naming.

## Another.Moniker

A role filed under the second naming.

## Misnamed.Title

A branch that answers to title, as a role does.

## Misnamed.Subhead

A role filed under the branch called title.

## Objective.Caption

A role named for what plays it.

## Labelled

Names its part with the role called caption.

## Nothing

Holds a part none of the time.

## Lopsided

Holds a part three times up to once.

## Flat

Holds a part two times up to twice.

## Redeclared

A button that declares a size, which every button has already.

## Doubly

A danger that declares its colour twice.
