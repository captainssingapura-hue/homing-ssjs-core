# A hypothetical house

## Saying

What a part tells the user about its owner.

## Doing

What a part lets the user do to its owner.

## Shaping

How a part makes up its owner's room.

## Naming

Tells what the owner is called.

## Telling

Tells something about the owner beyond its name.

## Scaling

Tells where along a scale the owner's value can lie.

## Going

Takes the user from the owner to somewhere else.

## Managing

Changes what the owner holds.

## Committing

Ends a decision the owner puts to the user.

## Title

Names a container, so the user knows what it holds.

## Name

Names a control or an item, so the user knows which one it is.

## Tag

Marks the owner with a short word that sorts it among others.

## Value

States the owner's value as it is now.

## Summary

Says in brief what the owner is about.

## Notch

Marks one step the owner's value can stop at.

## Open

Goes to the thing the owner stands for.

## Add

Adds one more to what the owner holds.

## Confirm

Does what the user decided.

## Cancel

Leaves without deciding anything.

## Entry

One of the things the owner lists.

## Control

Something the user operates to make a thing happen.

## Container

Something that holds others together, as one thing to the user.

## Text

Words the user reads.

## Mark

A small sign the user reads at a glance.

## Button

A control that does one thing when the user presses it.

## Card

A container that stands for one thing among others like it.

## Caption

Quiet words that say something beside what they are on.

## Heading

Words that say what a container holds.

## Badge

A short word that sorts what it is on.

## Tick

One step of a scale, marked.

## PlainButton

Does one ordinary thing, which nothing about it warns of.

## DangerButton

Does one thing that destroys, or cannot be undone.

## AlternatingButton

Does one thing whose risk changes: from safe to dangerous, as the risk it is shown says.

## PlainCard

Stands for one thing, and holds whatever its user puts in it.

## ProfileCard

Stands for one person: who they are, how they are sorted, and the way to them.

## Shelf

Holds what sorts a thing and the way to it, either of which may be missing.

## Confirmation

Puts a decision to the user, to be confirmed, or left if it may be.

## Watchlist

Keeps the people the user follows, and lets them follow one more.

## Gauge

Reads a value out along a scale of notches.
