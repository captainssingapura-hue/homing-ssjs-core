# A local-style branch, its virtual leaves, and what it refuses

## Region

A part of a page set apart.

## Frame

What surrounds a region's contents, drawn as a local style draws it.

## Edge

Where the frame ends.

## Mark

What marks the place being worked on.

## Box

A plain region, cast as any component is.

## Stray

An ordinary component where only virtual leaves may sit.

## Place

Where a part stands in its owner.

## Holder

Holds a part played by a virtual leaf.

## Partful

A virtual leaf that declares parts.

## SubFrame

A branch under a design branch.

## Deep

A component under the branch under the design branch.
