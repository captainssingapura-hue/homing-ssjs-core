# A hypothetical page

## Sheet

What the document itself wears: the page's whole surface, which nothing stands apart from.

## Frame

What stands on the page, with edges of its own.

## Ground

The colour and the ink of the page, worn by the document itself.

## Plain

A plain thing standing on the page.

## Busy

A ground with rooms, values and an axis, which a ground cannot have.

## Framing

How a part sits in its frame.

## Floor

The part a box stands on.

## Mat

A leaf the document wears, cast as a box's floor.

## Box

A frame with a floor.

## Bossy

A component that says the document wears it.
