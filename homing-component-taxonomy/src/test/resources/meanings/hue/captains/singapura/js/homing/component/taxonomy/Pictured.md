# A house of pictures

## Showing

What a part shows of its owner.

## Sort

Shows which way the owner's rows are ordered.

## Lead

Shows what the owner is, before its words.

## Mark

A small sign that stands for something.

## Picture

A mark drawn as a picture.

## Bar

A strip of things side by side.

## Up

A picture pointing up.

## Down

A picture pointing down.

## Star

A picture of a star.

## Header

The top of a table's column.

## Row

One line of a list.
