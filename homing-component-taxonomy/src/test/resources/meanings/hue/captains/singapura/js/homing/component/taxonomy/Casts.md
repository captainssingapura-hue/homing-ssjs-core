# Role sets, and the castings and sets the reading refuses

## Desk

Where keys and panels are kept.

## Acts

What a part does for its owner, with a look of its own.

## Press

Pressed to act.

## Hold

Held to act.

## Seating

Where a part only sits.

## Seat

Seats a part.

## Key

A thing pressed.

## Plain

A thing only placed.

## Panel

Holds a key pressed and a plain thing seated.

## Pusher

Casts a plain thing as pressed.

## Holder

Casts a key as held.

## Twice

A thing whose set names a role twice.

## Placed

A thing whose set names a role that only places a part.

## Idle

A thing whose set names no role.
