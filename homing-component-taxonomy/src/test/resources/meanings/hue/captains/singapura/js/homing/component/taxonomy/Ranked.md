# A hypothetical gear

## Gear

Parts of a machine the user handles.

## Acting

What a part does for its owner.

## Steer

Turns the owner.

## Mode

How a part of the gear stands.

## Faded

Shown dimmed.

## Pointed

The pointer is over it.

## Picked

Chosen among its kind.

## Armed

Ready to act.

## Grabbed

Held by the hand.

## Docked

Fixed in its place.

## Knob

A turning control.

## Dial

A control read by its face.

## Console

The gear's panel, a knob steering it.

## Unranked

A state of no level.

## Wide

A pseudo state of three values.

## Colonless

A pseudo-class without its colon.

## Muddled

A component that lists its states badly.
