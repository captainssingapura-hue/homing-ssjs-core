# A taxonomy with states

## Control

What the user operates.

## Leading

How a part leads its owner.

## Lead

Goes first among its owner's parts.

## Availability

Whether the user can use it, and how.

## Enabled

Usable.

## Hovered

The pointer is over it.

## Selected

Picked from among its kind.

## Keys

Where the page's keys are.

## Tab

One page of a set, picked to show it.

## Strip

A row of tabs.
